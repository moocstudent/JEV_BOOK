## Traps only the source reveals

This chapter gathers the negative information scattered across Laya's README, issues and source comments, because it decides your production outcome more than any headline number.

@fig t12-noul

## noul follows its labels (#156)

Internally `noul` renders its two options as the strings `false:` and `true:`, and that label pair can dominate the document, returning a confident no for input that clearly warrants yes. The remedy is to rewrite the question as a two-option `choice` with neutral keys `A`/`B` and the yes/no in the descriptions.

## Three more

- **score position bias** (#131): the multilingual checkpoint almost never picks the first level, in any language — route English score questions to the English checkpoint.
- **act_probability carries no signal** (#185): it reads 1.0 for nearly every input and its raw logits run against correctness at AUROC 0.30. Gate on `confidence` (AUROC 0.77), not on it.
- **the choice:11+ temperature bucket**: shipped at 0.1006, anti-calibration, refused by the library's own `TEMP_MIN=0.5` (see CA2).

Alongside these, an implementation detail this book found: the field named `confidence` is normalized entropy, not a probability (see CA1).

## One habit

None of these traps is on the landing page; they live in issues and source comments. Build a habit: before shipping an open model, `grep` its source for words like `TEMP_MIN`, `entropy`, `act_probability`, and read its open issues. That half hour saves you the first surprise after go-live.

**Exercise**: cross-check every noul question you have with a two-option choice. Rewrite the ones that disagree as choices.
